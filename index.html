My approach

A garden should feel as though it belongs.

Design begins with listening. I look at what is already there, what deserves to stay, how the garden connects with the home and how it will change through the seasons and over the years.

Listen firstUnderstand the people, the place and how the garden is actually lived in.
Work with what existsKeep worthwhile trees, plants and character rather than clearing for the sake of starting again.
Think beyond day oneCreate a garden that can mature, change and become better with time.

About Dan

A return to something first loved a long time ago.

I grew up in England and trained in horticulture and garden design before my working life took me through writing, advertising, creative work and other businesses. Returning to gardens now feels like a strange kind of circle — bringing the creative and practical sides together.

For me, the English touch is not about imposing an “English garden” everywhere. It is an attitude: horticultural curiosity, a feel for planting, respect for what is already there and the belief that a good garden should feel like part of the home.

“Styles come and go. Good places endure.”
